📱 Installation Guide

Follow these steps to install the Frontend Progress Wallpaper on your home screen

← Back to Wallpaper
🍎

For iOS (iPhone/iPad)

1

Open in Safari

Make sure you're using Safari browser. Chrome on iOS doesn't support "Add to Home Screen" as reliably.

2

Tap Share Button

Tap the Share icon (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom of Safari.

3

Scroll Down & Select

Scroll down in the share menu and tap "Add to Home Screen".

4

Name & Add

Name it "Dev Wallpaper" or keep default, then tap "Add" in top right.

Note: The icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it to open in fullscreen app mode.
🤖

For Android (Chrome)

1

Open in Chrome

Use Chrome browser for best experience.

2

Tap Menu (Three Dots)

Tap the three dots in top right corner to open menu.

3

Select "Add to Home screen"

Tap "Add to Home screen" from the menu.

4

Confirm Installation

Name it and tap "Add". The app will install automatically.

Tip: After installing, you can long-press the icon to resize widget if supported by your launcher.
